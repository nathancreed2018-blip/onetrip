OneTrip support

Help with your account, routes, saved trips and the iPhone beta.

Contact us

For help with OneTrip, email screed@rosesantiques.com.

If you are a beta tester, open OneTrip in TestFlight and choose Send Beta Feedback. Include your app version and a short description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

How do I create an account?

Choose Sign up, enter your email and a password, and confirm your email using the link we send. Then log in to save trips to your account. Your name is optional, and you can keep exploring as a guest. The planner and account pages are currently in English.

I cannot sign in or find my confirmation email

Check that you entered the right email address, and look in your spam or junk folder. Confirm your email before signing in. If you forgot your password, choose Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen, enter your account email, and follow the reset link sent to that address. Contact support if you still cannot access your account.

Where are my trips saved?

While signed in, saved trips sync to your account when connected. Log in to the same account in another account-enabled version of OneTrip to see them. Plans are also cached on your device. Older iPhone builds without an Account option still save only on that device; they do not gain sync automatically.

As a guest, plans stay in that browser or app on that device. Signing in does not automatically upload guest plans. Choose the option to import guest trips if you want to save them to your account. Clearing local storage before importing can remove those guest plans.

Will I receive marketing emails?

Only if you choose the optional marketing checkbox. It is unchecked by default. You can change that preference or opt out in Account. Emails needed to verify your address or reset your password are separate from marketing emails.

How do I change or delete a trip?

Open Trips in the planner to edit or delete an itinerary. Changes to account-saved trips sync when connected. Guest plans can be managed on the device where they are stored.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account, choose Delete account, enter your current password, and confirm the deletion. This permanently removes your OneTrip account and its cloud-saved trips. The app attempts to clear its account cache on the current device. Other devices may retain local copies; clear their browser site storage or app data to remove those copies.

Guest plans are separate and can be deleted locally. Signing out hides account plans but may retain the local account cache for unsynced changes. Signing out, deleting the app, or choosing Reset guest data does not itself delete your cloud account. If you cannot sign in to delete it, use password reset or contact support for help.

Can I book flights, hotels or rental cars?

The homepage’s Search on Expedia button opens flight and stay searches. Find rental cars opens DiscoverCars. These companies and their travel providers handle live prices, availability, reservations and payments. OneTrip does not make reservations or accept payments. Planner estimates remain illustrative.

OneTrip is an affiliate of Expedia and DiscoverCars and may earn a commission on eligible bookings made through these links. The links do not include your saved trip or profile data. For a reservation, change, payment or refund, contact the provider that handled your booking. Provider websites may use referral cookies under their own privacy policies.

How does TestFlight work?

OneTrip is available to invited internal testers. External testers receive access after Apple approves the build for external testing. Open that invitation on your iPhone and follow the TestFlight installation instructions. Available features depend on the build installed.

Will it work offline?

The installed iPhone app can plan and save locally offline. Account sign-in, email confirmation, password resets and syncing require a connection, as may map imagery. The website needs a connection to load. Reconnect to sync changes before relying on a plan on another device.
